About Me

My name is Anthony Munenga, and I am a second-year student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Cyber Security at Mulungushi University, in the School of Engineering and Technology. I am passionate about technology, problem solving, and understanding how systems can be protected from threats. Outside of my studies, I enjoy music, playing the trumpet, and spending time on the soccer field. Through this course, I am learning how websites are built from the ground up using HTML, CSS, and eventually JavaScript, which I see as a valuable skill alongside my cyber security training, since understanding how the web works helps me understand how it can be secured.
